About Us

The story, the vision and the people behind the work

The Foundation was registered in November 2022 and fully certified by the Department of Social Welfare with a Certificate of Recognition as a non-profit organisation on 29th May 2023. It operates as a Community-Based Organisation in Awutu Bereku in the Senya District, Central Region of Ghana.

The Founder realised the need to train vulnerable youth and women in communities so they could obtain employable skills and fend for their families. He finds this approach sustainable because his late sister benefited from similar training given by an elderly woman in her church who had retired from her work as a baker. That woman took it upon herself to provide skilful training in pastries and bread-making to women and youth who lacked employable skills, specifically in bakery. The late sister took advantage of this opportunity, developed the skills, set up a bakery and employed workers. In addition, she extended support to vulnerable families for their daily sustenance.

This impact necessitated the birth of Gloryland Realty Foundation (GRF) in order to provide these employable vocational skills to the needy and vulnerable in deprived communities. The office is located at the former GN Bank, Awutu Bereku, CW-0001-2142 Sunbird Street. The Foundation has three Directors and two Board members who steer the affairs of the organisation.

Today Gloryland Realty Foundation continues that legacy. We work to reduce unemployment among youth and women and to empower the vulnerable in society. We cannot do this alone and that is why we invite partners, volunteers and supporters to walk with us.

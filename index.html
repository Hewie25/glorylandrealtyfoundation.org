What we hope to achieve

Our Vision

To ensure that all the less privileged in society have various skills to trade with, as a means of earning income, thereby eliminating liability and vulnerability in society.

Our Mission

1. To create vocational job opportunities for people in deprived communities, especially the youth, increasing the income of individuals and the community.
2. To ensure that the vulnerable and the less privileged in society have a skilled job to trade with, as a means of earning income in the community.



Our objectives as an organization

Skills Training

To reach one hundred thousand (100,000) vulnerable people, especially women and single mothers living in deprived community areas, with vocational skill training as a means of income - eliminating poverty, reducing the unemployment rate and liability in our society.

Community Focus

To help the poor, the needy, the less privileged and the vulnerable living in deprived community areas receive full vocational skilled training so they can trade for a better life.

Lasting Independence

To create opportunities for self-employment in the community and society, thereby increasing self-employment across the nation.
